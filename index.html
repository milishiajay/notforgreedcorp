Profit, Not Greed

We're a different kind of company. Every decision answers to the same order: customers, employees, community, then growth.

  • Customers first — fair prices, no upsells, no hidden fees. Ever.
  • Employees second — fair wages plus profit sharing. The people who do the work share in the win.
  • Community third — a share of every dollar flows back into local programs and local hiring.
  • Growth fourth — we are not a charity. Profit funds everything above. Greed is the only thing off the table.
  • Open books — our clients and our workers can see where money comes from and where it goes.
  • The standard — as we grow, we only do business with suppliers who meet our ethical standard. Good business begets good business.
